<p><b>URL Radio</b> is a minimalist app for listening to radio over the Internet. Free, open source and without ads or tracking – no frills, just radio.</p>

<p><b>Listening</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Plays MP3, AAC, Ogg (Vorbis / Opus), FLAC and HLS streams</li>
<li>Shows the current song for Icecast / Shoutcast, HLS and Ogg streams, with a history you can page through and copy</li>
<li>Sleep timer</li>
<li>Home screen widget and quick settings tile</li>
<li>Android Auto and voice commands ("Play … on URL Radio")</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Recording</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Record what is playing with one tap – long-press for a recording with a timer</li>
<li>AAC (.m4a), Opus (.ogg, Android 10+) or lossless WAV, with selectable bit rate</li>
<li>Recordings are saved to Music/URL Radio on Android 10 and newer</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Finding and organising stations</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Search the <a href="https://www.radio-browser.info/">radio-browser.info</a> database – the most listened-to stations first, with logo and bit rate</li>
<li>Add stations from M3U / M3U8 / PLS playlists, by entering a stream address, or by tapping a stream link in your browser</li>
<li>Favourites (swipe right), tags (#jazz in the station name) and an optional search in your station list</li>
<li>Set a station logo from the gallery or from a web address</li>
<li>Export your stations as M3U / PLS, back them up and restore them</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Design</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Material 3 Expressive with dynamic colours, light and dark theme</li>
<li>Two-pane layout on tablets and foldables</li>
<li>Translated into more than 30 languages</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Android Auto:</b> URL Radio is not distributed through Google Play. To use it in the car, enable "Unknown sources" in the Android Auto developer settings (tap the version number in the Android Auto settings ten times to unlock them).</p>

<p>Missing a station? Help the radio-browser.info community by <a href="https://www.radio-browser.info/add">adding it to their database</a>.</p>
